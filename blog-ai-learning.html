Using AI as a Learning Partner

AI can explain a topic in different ways, but real learning still comes from checking, practising, and explaining the idea yourself.

Start with a focused question

Instead of asking for an entire subject at once, ask about one concept, request a simple example, and then ask what changes when the input changes. For programming, trace the example line by line before running it.

Verify the answer

AI can make mistakes or leave out important details. Compare important claims with official documentation, course materials, or a working test. If an explanation and the result disagree, investigate rather than memorising the answer.

Turn explanations into practice

  • Write a short summary without looking at the answer.
  • Solve a similar problem independently.
  • Ask for feedback on your reasoning, not only the final output.
  • Keep notes about what confused you and how you resolved it.
Use AI to support your thinking—not as a substitute for testing, reading source material, or doing the work yourself.
